Set up Google sign-in

This copy of Dubdeck doesn't have a Google sign-in ID yet. Making one is free and takes about ten minutes.

  1. Open the Google Cloud console and create a project (any name).
  2. Turn on the YouTube Data API v3 for that project.
  3. Open Google Auth Platform, choose Get started, pick External, then under Audience › Test users add the Gmail address you'll sign in with. Without this, Google says “Access blocked”.
  4. Under Clients, create a Web application client and add to Authorized JavaScript origins.
  5. Copy the client ID and paste it here.

Add your Spotify playlists

Paste a link and Dubdeck reads the songs. No Spotify login needed, and nothing is sent to YouTube Music until you choose Transfer.

Paste a Spotify link

  • One playlist: open it in Spotify, then ••• › Share › Copy link to playlist. The whole playlist comes over.
  • All your playlists: open your Spotify profile, then ••• › Copy link to profile. Dubdeck lists every playlist shown on your profile so you can tick the ones to copy.

No link handy? to see how matching works. Nothing is sent to YouTube unless you choose to.

Private playlists and Liked Songs

Spotify only shares public playlists by link. The quickest fix for a private playlist is ••• › Make public, paste its link above, then make it private again. Liked Songs and anything you'd rather keep private can come in one of these ways.

Copy the songs

  1. In the Spotify desktop app, open the playlist or Liked Songs.
  2. Click a song, press Ctrl+A to select them all, then Ctrl+C (on a Mac, ⌘A and ⌘C).
  3. Paste into the box above and press Add. You can rename the playlist before it's transferred.

Upload a file

Adds every playlist in the file at once.

  • Exportify CSV from exportify.net: instant, with every playlist and Liked Songs.
  • TuneMyMusic CSV export.
  • Spotify data download (Playlist1.json, YourLibrary.json), requested under Spotify privacy settings. It arrives by email within a few days; unzip it first.
